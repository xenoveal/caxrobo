# Highest Probability Trade Checklist

- ✅ Trend agrees (Higher High / Lower Low)
- ✅ Chart pattern confirms
- ✅ Break of Structure (BoS)
- ✅ Strong volume on breakout
- ✅ RSI/MACD confirmation
- ✅ Support/Resistance confluence
- ✅ Fibonacci confluence
- ✅ No major nearby resistance/support
- ✅ Risk:Reward ≥ 1:2
- ✅ Higher timeframe aligns (HTF confirmation)

The more confirmations present, the higher the probability of a successful trade.
